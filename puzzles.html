How this works

  • Click a piece — every square it can legally go to lights up with a dot.
  • Illegal moves are simply refused: you can't leave your own king in check, and castling, en passant and promotion all work properly.
  • Get it right and the board says so. Get it wrong and you can Undo and try again.
  • Pawns reaching the last row let you pick what to promote to.
