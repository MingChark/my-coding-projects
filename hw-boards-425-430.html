Real boards. Try each one, then watch the answer play out.

Handed out around 12 Aug 2026 👨‍🏫 Covered in the 15 Aug class Engine-verified

Six positions from the Russian puzzle book. Each one has a game and a year under it on the paper sheet — these are real positions from real games.

How to use a board. Click a piece and its legal squares light up — then click one to move. Only legal moves are allowed.

Press ▶ Show the answer and the whole line plays itself, one move at a time. Press it again to pause. Next move steps through by hand instead, and you can click any move in the list to jump straight to it.

Check the little triangle on the paper sheet to see who moves — some of these are Black to play, which catches people out.

Every position was checked with a chess engine before it went on this page: legal position, and the answer line confirmed.
