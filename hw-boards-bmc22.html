Last year's sheet — every puzzle on a real board

Handed out ~24 Aug 2025 Engine-verified

Seven of the nine, in the sheet's three rows: MATE, MATERIAL and BEST MOVE.

How to use a board. Click a piece and its legal squares light up — then click one to move. Only legal moves are allowed.

Press ▶ Show the answer and the whole line plays itself, one move at a time. Press it again to pause. Next move steps through by hand instead.

The MATE ones end in a real checkmate. The MATERIAL ones win a piece — usually by giving something up first and forking. The BEST MOVE ones are the hardest: sometimes there's only one move that holds.

Every position was solved from scratch with a chess engine and checked legal. The paper sheet is on the homework page.
