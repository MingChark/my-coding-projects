♟️ Two games, two wins

Coach Dong goes through JJ's last two online games, move by move

📅 Reviews of 31 Oct and 7 Nov 2026 👨‍🏫 Coach Dong (董老师) Rating 1622 → 1614 · both won

Two weeks, two wins — one with Black against a 1104, one with White against a 1717, which is 103 points above JJ. Coach Dong went through both on his screen, and the boards below are his move lists with his comments attached to the exact moves he said them about.

Two things he'd change. Both are about move order, not about tactics:

“先出马。我们一般是先出马。Jason 在这里先出马，会好一点。”
Knights out first. We usually develop the knights first. Jason, getting the knight out here would have been a bit better.
📅 7 Nov 2026 · game review, 33:35 — about 9.Bf4

And on move 14 he wanted the queen, not the rook: “我喜欢后 E2” — I like Qe2. The board marks the spot.

The move he liked. 11.Nb5! got a “好棋” — good move. It came straight after the opponent's 10…Be6?!, which Coach said should have been …Rb8 precisely because of Nb5.

And one small piece of advice for the attack: at move 29, with the queens still on, “给自己开个小窗” — give your king a little window. That is what 29.g3 is for.

⭐ 25 Oct · JJ with Black

D44 · Queen's Gambit Accepted · Chess.com rapid 30 min · 0–1

JJ took the gambit pawn on move two. Coach Dong's note: “这也是一个很常见的应对” — a perfectly normal way to answer it.

▶ Live board.

The move of the game, in his words:

“马 F4 —— 漂亮。”
Knight to f4 — beautiful.
📅 31 Oct 2026 · game review, 02:45 — about 18…Nf4

And right after it, the opponent's 19.b4?: “这步走的比较鲁莽” — rather reckless. Coach wanted 19.Re3, defending the knight, instead. After b4 the pawn itself became the problem and both of JJ's knights came crashing in.

What he'd have done differently as Black. On move 6, instead of castling: “Jason 可以走 …c5 来冲击对方的 d4 中心” — …c5 to hit the d4 centre. JJ played it a move later anyway, and Coach called that “好棋” too.

The piece that won it. “关键对方的这个 b7 象， 特别的强” — that bishop on b7 was especially strong. It is pointing at the white king's corner from move 15 to the end.

The board above stops at move 23. A few moves later White missed a knight fork and resigned.

🎯 What to take away

  • Knights before bishops. Coach said it about 9.Bf4 and it is the general rule.
  • …Rb8 before …Be6 when a knight can jump to b5 — the opponent's mistake was JJ's chance.
  • Give your king a window when you attack with the queen. g3.
  • Hit the centre with …c5 in the Queen's Gambit Accepted rather than drifting.
  • Don't push pawns in front of your own pieces — 19.b4? left the pawn weak and the knight loose.
  • A bishop on the long diagonal is worth keeping. The b7 bishop decided the 25 Oct game.
