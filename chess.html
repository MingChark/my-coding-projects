🎯 The six things to practise

Coach Li's greatest hits
  1. Don't give away the centre.
  2. Before you push a pawn, check your pieces are safe.
  3. If you sacrifice, work out what happens when they don't take.
  4. Drill skewers.
  5. Never trade just because you can.
  6. In the endgame, march your king up and make a passed pawn.

How this works. Each Saturday the coach posts the pairing table to WeChat after class. The game has to be played by Thursday — after that there's no time for the review coach to go through it. Games are 30 min + 5 sec on Chess.com.
