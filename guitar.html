How these pages work

The music is on the page, marked. Each lesson shows the actual sheet, with the stretches Teacher Bowen talked about highlighted right on the staff. Tap a highlighted stretch and it jumps to what he said about those exact bars — and tap the number next to a comment to jump back to the music.

Everything is quoted. The comments come straight off the lesson recording, with the time it was said, so nothing is second-hand. Close-ups of the bars sit next to each one.

The pencil on the music is Jason's own, written during the lesson — note names, fingerings, and the circles Teacher Bowen asked for.
