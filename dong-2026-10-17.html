JJ wins a piece before the opening is over

📅 Sat 17 Oct 2026 👨‍🏫 Coach Dong (董老师) 30 minutes · eight games reviewed

⭐ JJ's game

C42 Petrov's Defence · JJ had White · 1–0 · over in the opening

It finished almost before it started — and Coach Dong still picked it out as one worth everyone studying.

“这盘棋下得很快，开局的时候就结束了。但是我觉得这个开局，是值得我们小朋友再去学习一下的。”
This game went very fast — it was over in the opening. But I think the opening is worth the rest of you going back and studying.
📅 17 Oct 2026 · game review, 07:47

What happened. It was a Petrov: 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nf6 3.Nxe5 d6 4.Nf3 Nxe4. JJ then chose 5.Qe2 — which Coach Dong called a calm, solid choice — and that queen move quietly pins the black knight on e4 against the king. His opponent played a natural-looking move without noticing, JJ leaned on the pinned piece, and the knight simply fell off.

“习惯性的随手棋……忽略了这实际上是一个牵制。”
A habit move, played without thinking — and it missed that this is a pin.
📅 17 Oct 2026 · game review, 08:24

Put pressure on the pinned piece — Coach Dong said it in English, and that is the whole lesson. A pinned piece can't run, so the way to win it is to attack it again.

📖 What Black had to play

C42 · the move that holds everything together

If you ever have the black side of this, Coach Dong was emphatic: when White plays Qe2, you have to answer …Qe7. Blocking with the queen is the only move.

“当对手走后 E2 之后，就说你一定要走后 E7。一定要走。”
When your opponent plays Qe2, you absolutely must play Qe7. You must.
📅 17 Oct 2026 · game review, 08:44

▶ Live board. Play it through — the queens come off and the position goes symmetrical:

Why strong players like the Petrov: it heads straight for this sort of balanced, symmetrical position, and it is an easy game to hold. “高水平棋手是黑方，应对王前兵的时候，很喜欢走彼得洛夫” — strong players as Black love it against 1.e4.

⚔️ If you'd rather have a fight

C42 · 5.Nc3 instead of 5.Qe2 · for White

The honest problem with the Petrov is that it tends to a draw. Coach Dong offered the other road for White: 5.Nc3, trade on c3, recapture towards the centre with dxc3, and the d-file opens up for a long-castling attack.

▶ Live board. Castle long and throw the h-pawn:

He was careful about this. It does not mean White is better — only that the game gets complicated and there is something to attack. “对于一些喜欢进攻的小棋手而言” — if you're the sort who likes attacking, it's worth knowing.

🎯 What to take away

  • A pinned piece can't run — so hit it again. That is how JJ won this game in the opening.
  • Don't play habit moves. The opponent's move here was natural and lost a piece on the spot.
  • Against Qe2 in the Petrov, …Qe7 is forced. Learn that one.
  • If the symmetrical Petrov bores you as White, 5.Nc3 and castle long.
